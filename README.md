# 8H-News — The Official Chaos Bureau of Bathurst High
8H‑News is Bathurst High’s fastest, loudest, and most dangerously honest student‑run news outlet. We deliver instant, unfiltered coverage of every dramatic moment, questionable decision, and legendary disaster as it unfolds in real time across the halls of BHS. No corporate sponsors, no censorship, no teachers telling us to “tone it down” — just raw, uncut stories straight from the source.

Our reporters operate in the shadows, gathering intel from anonymous witnesses, hallway lurkers, and students who swear they “didn’t see anything” but definitely did. We expose the events that matter — the scandals, the victories, the downfalls, the beef, the memes — all documented with the seriousness of a real newsroom and the energy of a group chat at 2 AM.

We don’t twist the truth.
We don’t sugarcoat the chaos.
We don’t rewrite history.

Headlines are final.  
Stories are real (mostly).  
And if something happens at Bathurst High, 8H‑News is already reporting it.

Welcome to the only news outlet brave enough to cover the things everyone else is too scared, too confused, or too responsible to publish.
