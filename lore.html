“The Two‑Day Ghost of Bathurst High”

Is he even real??

FABI

LORE OF FABI

Long before Bathurst High became the chaotic ecosystem it is today, there existed a student whose presence was so rare, so fleeting, so unbelievably inconsistent that he became less of a person and more of a rumour carried by the wind. His name was Fabi — though he insists on being called Lucas — and he is known across the school as The Two‑Day Ghost. 🌑 CHAPTER I — The Vanishing Begins Lucas enrolled at Bathurst High with the same optimism as everyone else. He showed up on Day 1, backpack full, uniform crisp, ready to face the year. Then he disappeared. Not like “took a sick day.” Not like “went on holiday.” No — he vanished with the commitment of a man who decided school was optional and life was a side quest. Weeks passed. Months passed. Teachers whispered. Students speculated. The attendance system gave up and wrote “???”. Then, out of nowhere, he reappeared for Day 2 — walked into class like nothing happened, sat down, nodded at the teacher, and then vanished again for the rest of the year. 🌒 CHAPTER II — Theories of His Whereabouts With no sightings, no explanations, and no proof he even existed, Bathurst High began forming theories. Theory 1 — The Couch Sink Some say Lucas sank into his couch one afternoon and achieved perfect comfort, becoming physically unable to leave. He now lives in a dimension made entirely of cushions and half‑finished Netflix shows. Theory 2 — The Side Quest Arc Others believe he’s been completing side quests no one else has unlocked: befriending stray cats mastering obscure video games learning forbidden recipes becoming a part‑time philosopher discovering new sleep schedules that defy human biology Theory 3 — The Shadow Organisation A more dramatic theory claims Lucas runs a secret underground operation from his bedroom, controlling events at Bathurst High from afar. He is the unseen puppet master, the architect of chaos, the man who knows everything but appears nowhere. Theory 4 — The Time Zone Incident Some swear he accidentally shifted into a different timezone entirely and has been living three days behind everyone else. He tries to come to school, but arrives on the wrong date every time. 🌓 CHAPTER III — The School’s Reaction Teachers have stopped calling his name during roll. Students refer to him as “a concept.” The office staff list him as “Missing, presumed vibing.” Even the school system can’t track him. His attendance record looks like a glitch in the matrix. When someone mentions Lucas, people respond with: “He goes here?” “I thought he moved.” “I heard he ascended.” 🌕 CHAPTER IV — The Legend Grows Despite never being present, Lucas has become one of the most iconic figures in Bathurst High lore. His absence is so powerful it has become a personality trait. He is the anti‑hero of attendance. The ghost of Year 8. The myth who refuses to be bound by timetables. Some say he will return one day. Some say he will appear for a third day, causing a school‑wide celebration. Some say he will descend from the heavens, wearing his uniform like a prophecy fulfilled. But until that day comes, Lucas remains what he has always been: A legend built not by presence, but by absence.
